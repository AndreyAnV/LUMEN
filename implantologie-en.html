COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers in clear language.

How long does treatment take?

The timeline depends on healing, available bone volume and the procedures required. Your dentist can provide an estimate after the assessment.

Does the price include the crown?

The starting price covers the dental implant. Diagnostic tests, the abutment, crown and any additional procedures are itemised separately in the treatment plan.

What does aftercare involve?

Daily oral hygiene and regular reviews are important. Recommendations and recall intervals are tailored to each situation.
